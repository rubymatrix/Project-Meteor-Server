# Arc200: Filling the Quiver

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Arc/Arc200.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Arc/Arc200.lua)

Quest 110160. Archer class quest, level 20; the client has no prereq gating.

- SEQ_ACCEPT: offered by Nonolato (Gridania).
- SEQ_000: Keelty's briefing; it hands over the Quiver's Hold linkpearl.
- SEQ_010: meet Keelty's party at the North Shroud fence (push).
- SEQ_020: drive the yarzon invaders from Fallgourd (SimpleContentArc20001, QuestDirectorArc20001).
- SEQ_030: Nonolato's reward.

Every push on the fence ends in the join prompt, so a declined or lost battle is entered again from
there. Spawns, pools and class fills: `Data/sql/seed/seed_quest_arc200_01.sql`.
