# Man402: Of Men They Sing

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Man/Man402.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Man/Man402.lua)

Quest 110018, after Lord Errant (Man308), level 42.

- SEQ_000, held: Lord Errant's payout grants the quest silently (`player:AddQuest`); a "???" plea
  repeats on the Path of the Twelve pearl. Tataru in the Waking Sands offers it: pES (line 249, the
  accept window, then man40200 mode 2 and its result; a decline returns the window's 2), then
  SEQ_001. A character who finished Lord Errant without the hand-on gets the same offer at
  SEQ_ACCEPT (65535).
- SEQ_001, Camp Nine Ivies (East Shroud): the push asks, the first time plays pE10 (man40210, a8 =
  the companion's dead relative), then the search duty, SEQ_005.
- SEQ_005, the search duty (content SimpleContentMan40201, QuestDirectorMan40201): the companion
  leads down the Nine Ivies gullies to the wounded scout; two bloodhounds. Win: pE20 (man40220),
  SEQ_015 on the scene's own mark. A loss keeps SEQ_005 and the camp push relaunches the duty
  without the scene.
- SEQ_015, Tataru: pE30 (man40230, after warp) out to an Ul'dah street, where the after-warp notice
  pays the reward window and completes.

Journal text keys on 0-4, 5-14 and 15. pES hands a4 straight to the cutscene (no
getSnpcActorClassID), so it takes the companion's class id.
