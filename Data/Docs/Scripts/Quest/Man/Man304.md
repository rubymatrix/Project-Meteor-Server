# Man304: Forever Taken

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Man/Man304.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Man/Man304.lua)

Forever Taken (Man304, quest 110016). Follows Toll of the Warden (Man300); level 34.

Toll of the Warden's turn-in hands this quest on silently; Hedyn's pES is the accept. The client's
journal tests 0, 5, 10-19, 20 and 25, so the held quest sits at 1 (no paragraph) and the accepted
one moves to 0.

| Seq | Step |
|---|---|
| 1 | Held. Zoxio's call on the Ashcrown pearl. Hedyn in the Peasants Ward (report copy, PA 2): pES (1 = accepted); the soil asks for one element, rolled then (1-6: fire, ice, wind, earth, lightning, water). |
| 0 | The five Silvertear soil points in Mor Dhona: each asks to bury a crystal of the element (processEvent000_20), drains it for five minutes, then gives an Unaspected Crystal (journal int2). The fifth moves to 5. |
| 5 | Hedyn takes the crystals (pE10); the pearl lights with his summons. |
| 10 | The Ul'dah market entrance into the Waking Sands (181 PA 12); the main hall stairs (push): pE20, the assembly (content SimpleContentMan30401, QuestDirectorEventMan30401). |
| 20 | The companion in the hall: pE30, out to the Ul'dah street. |
| 25 | The reward as the player lands (onNotice); Minfilia pays it in the Sands if that was lost. |

The Peasants Ward is zone 161 when the server has it; until then its copies stand in 181 PA 13/14.
The five soil points share one class (`m3g_man304_spot1..5`).
