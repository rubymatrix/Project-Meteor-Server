# Man206: Together We Stand

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Man/Man206.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Man/Man206.lua). Quest 110014,
after Fade to White (Man200, 110013), level 22.

Fade to White's turn-in hands this quest on silently; its accept is Minfilia's `processEvent001`.

- Seq 0: held, no journal paragraph. Tataru's Path pearl calls the player in. The Ul'dah market
  entrance walks into this quest's Waking Sands (181 PA 11, the sylphs in the Solar). At level 22
  the west office door plays the sylphs' arrival (`processEventUdowntownrectStart`) once and arms
  Minfilia, whose `processEvent001` asks (1 = accepted).
- Seq 5: the companion's farewell on the Path pearl (one pack per personality); its last line moves
  to 10.
- Seq 10: the rendezvous at the Gridania aetheryte plaza (push): `pE12`.
- Seq 15: Camp Nine Ivies (push): the join ask, then `pE13` (the sylphs' plea, first time only),
  then the grove duty (content SimpleContentMan20601, QuestDirectorMan20601).
- Seq 20: in the grove: `processEvent016` (HQ MAN20610) near the grove's mouth, then Dokixia hands
  over the podling (`pE20`).
- Seq 25: carrying the podling back to Flaxio: `pE30`, out to the camp.
- Seq 30: Tataru in the Waking Sands: `processEvent040`, then the reward on the street outside.

The duty is a stealth run (QuestDirectorMan20601). Imperial sentries see the player at 10 yalms (3
while the sylphs' magicks hold). The companion walks along and warns of them; a talk with a sylph
renews the cover. A failed run (time up, the companion left behind) returns the step to 15 at the
camp, and the camp trigger asks again without the plea.
