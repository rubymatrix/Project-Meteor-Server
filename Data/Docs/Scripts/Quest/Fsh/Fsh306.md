# Fsh306: Polishing the Mast

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Fsh/Fsh306.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Fsh/Fsh306.lua)

Polishing the Mast (Fsh306, 110502). Follows Fsh300; offered by N'nmulika at the Fishermen's Guild
in Limsa Lominsa.

- Seq 0: the stairs at the rear of the guild (push).
- Seq 5: N'nmulika's timed fish assignment and the sale.
- Seq 20: N'nmulika's thanks for the sale.
- Seq 25: the Echoes on Sisipu (twice) and Maisie.
- Seq 28: N'nmulika's report and reward.

The assignment is rolled once and stored (counter 0 = slot in `ASSIGNABLE_FISH`, counter 1 = how many
of it the player owned then), so a reload never rerolls or extends it. Counter 2 holds the assignment
time in 16-second ticks (wrapping at 65536, about 12 days): the quest data has no wider field that is
saved. Spawns, gates and class fills: Data/sql/seed/seed_quest_fsh306_01.sql (Sisipu's public spawn
comes from seed_quest_fsh300_01.sql).
