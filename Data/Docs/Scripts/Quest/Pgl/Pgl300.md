# Pgl300: Here There Be Pirates

Script: [`Data/scripts/Quest/Pgl/Pgl300.lua`](../../../../scripts/Quest/Pgl/Pgl300.lua)

Quest 110061. Pugilist level 30, after The House Always Wins. Offered by Gagaruna in Ul'dah.

- SEQ_000: Waekbyrt in Limsa Lominsa (230); the Platinum Mirage linkpearl comes with his scene.
- SEQ_020: Mytesyn's Bronze Kraken Key (133).
- SEQ_030: the boarding of the Kraken (QuestDirectorPgl30001, content SimpleContentPgl30001, zone
  230). A lost boarding keeps SEQ_030 and Mytesyn's talk asks again. The win gives the counterfeit
  chip mold.
- SEQ_040: Titinin inspects the mold.
- SEQ_050: Titinin's verdict.
- SEQ_060: the Echoes of Hurrey, Melisie and Halstein, any order (flags 0-2); the last opens SEQ_090.
- SEQ_090: Gagaruna's reward.

Titinin's scenes fade in only after a warp, so each re-places the player beside him. The key and the
mold are this quest's props and leave with it. Spawns: `Data/sql/seed/seed_quest_pgl300_01.sql`.
