# PopulaceCompanyGuide: Grand Company representatives

Script: [`Data/scripts/Chara/Npc/Populace/PopulaceCompanyGuide.lua`](../../../../../scripts/Chara/Npc/Populace/PopulaceCompanyGuide.lua)

Client functions:

- `eventTalkWelcome()`: dialog for new recruits.
- `eventTalkProvisional()`: probably the message for a rank that isn't high enough.
- `eventTalkExclusive()`: message for a member of another Grand Company.
- `eventTalkComMember(nil, npc, isFoundationDay)`: information menus on Grand Company activities.
- `eventTalkStepBreak()`: returns the NPC to its neutral state.
